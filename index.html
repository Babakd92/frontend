HydroinformatiX

Explore, analyze, and understand hydrology data.

HydroinformatiX is a chat assistant built for questions about water, weather, and climate. Instead of guessing from memory or searching the open web, it pulls live figures from trusted sources such as USGS, NASA, NOAA, USDA, and EPA, along with satellite records. Every answer comes with a cited source, and usually a table, chart, or map showing exactly where the numbers came from.

Ask about a single location, such as a city or an address, or about an entire river watershed. Answers can draw on historical records, near real-time conditions, or climate projections decades into the future, depending on what you need.

What it can help with

  • Weather and climate: current conditions, recent history, and multi-decade climate outlooks for a city or an entire river watershed.
  • Rivers and groundwater: streamflow, river water level, groundwater depth, and short-term flood forecasts.
  • Drought: whether a place is currently in drought, and how that compares to its own history.
  • Water quality: measured nitrate, phosphorus, turbidity, and other pollutant levels in a watershed, and whether rainfall is statistically linked to them.
  • Land and agriculture: crop types, soil characteristics, expected storm runoff, and where conservation practices like cover crops would help most.
  • Watershed-scale conditions: evapotranspiration, soil moisture, vegetation health, and precipitation, averaged across an entire river basin rather than a single point.
